Tray panel components: `AlarmAudio` (a Web Audio alarm that escalates until it is muted), `ErrorMessage` (copy and settings buttons, `error` and `neutral` variants), `ConnectionStatus` (connection state with `unstable`, `forbidden` and `alert`), `CounterPanel` (brand, state and animated counters, with alert per counter, loading, error and no permission) and `TrayBoardScreen` (the panel with the alarm, the sound and settings buttons and the `width` event). New utilities `utils/earcon` and `utils/user-interaction`. Every visible text comes from the props, with English defaults.
